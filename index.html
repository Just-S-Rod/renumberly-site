Renumberly Support

What Renumberly does

Renumberly numbers the headings in your Google Docs™ document in one click (1, 1.1, 1.1.1). Click again after you edit to renumber, or remove the numbers in one click. As a bonus, a sidebar lists all your headings so you can jump to any of them.

It works only with the document you have open, while it's running. It runs inside Google Apps Script on Google's servers. We don't send your document content to any server we control, and we don't store your document text. Details: https://renumberly.com/privacy.html

Install

  1. In Google Docs, go to Extensions → Add-ons → Get add-ons and search for Renumberly.
  2. Click Install and choose your Google account.
  3. Review the two permissions (this document only, and showing a sidebar), then click Allow.
  4. Open a document, or reload one that's already open.

Work or school account? Your administrator may need to allow Marketplace apps first.

How to use

  1. Give your section titles heading styles: Format → Paragraph styles → Heading 1, 2, 3…. Renumberly only numbers real headings, not text that's just bold or large.
  2. Go to Extensions → Renumberly → Show sidebar.
  3. Click Number headings. Every heading gets a number by level: 1, 1.1, 1.1.1 …
  4. Added, deleted, or moved a section? Click Number headings again. Everything is renumbered from scratch, with no doubled numbers.
  5. To take the numbers off, click Remove. Your headings go back to their original text.
  6. The sidebar lists your headings and keeps the list up to date. Click a heading to jump to it, or type in the filter box to find one.

FAQ

My headings aren't numbered or listed. Make sure each one uses a heading style (Heading 1–6), then click the refresh button in the sidebar. The document title and subtitle are never numbered, on purpose.

Why does a heading show "1.0.1"? That heading skips a level, for example a Heading 3 placed directly under a Heading 1. The 0 marks the missing level. Change it to a Heading 2 if you want 1.1.

Will it mess up numbers I typed myself? Not unless you ask it to. By default, Renumberly only changes the numbers it added, and text like "2024 Plans" or "1. Intro" is left alone. If you want typed numbers replaced, tick Remove old typed numbers under Numbering options and tools. It's off by default. When it's on, a number typed at the start of a heading, like "1. Intro" or "6.3 Results", is replaced with Renumberly's number. Plain text like "2024 Plans" is still kept.

Does Remove keep my formatting? Yes. Bold, links, and colors on the heading stay.

Can I choose which levels get numbers, or start at Heading 2? Yes. Open Numbering options and tools in the sidebar and pick the range, for example H1 to H3, or H2 to H6 to leave chapter titles unnumbered.

Can I drop the trailing period or use other formats (like I, A, 1)? You can turn the trailing period on or off under Numbering options and tools (1.1. or 1.1). Other styles like I, A, 1 aren't available yet.

Does it renumber on its own while I type? Docs doesn't let add-ons react to edits, so outside the sidebar, nothing changes until you click Number headings. If you tick Renumbers while this sidebar is open, Renumberly renumbers new or moved headings after a short pause, as long as the sidebar stays open. It waits until you stop typing, so it won't rewrite a heading while you're in it. The setting is saved per document.

Can it spot a Heading 3 with no Heading 2 above it? Yes. Click Check heading levels under Numbering options and tools. It lists any skipped levels, and clicking one jumps to it.

I have numbering left by other add-ons. Click Clean up old numbers under Numbering options and tools. It shows a preview of what it will remove before anything changes.

What about headings in tables, headers and footers, or document tabs? Headings inside tables in the main body are numbered. Headings in headers, footers, or extra document tabs aren't covered yet.

Does it work on my phone or tablet? No. Like other add-ons of this type, it runs in Docs on a computer.

Docs already has a table of contents and an outline. Why use this? Those are great for navigation, and Renumberly doesn't replace them. What Docs doesn't do is number your headings. That's the main thing Renumberly adds.

Why did the old table-of-contents add-on I used stop working? We don't know, and we aren't connected to its developer. Renumberly is a separate add-on built independently. If you used the old one, we suggest uninstalling it so the two don't get confused in your Extensions menu.

Do you see or store my document? It runs inside Google Apps Script on Google's servers. We don't send your document content to any server we control, and we don't store your document text. It saves only your numbering settings and short coded fingerprints of the numbers it added, inside the document itself. Details: https://renumberly.com/privacy.html

Is it really free? What's the catch? Yes, every feature is free. There's no paid tier, no trial, and no license key. Renumberly is an experiment in a small product built and run by a team of AI bots with one human owner, and the point is the experiment, not the money.

How do I uninstall? If you want the numbers gone, click Remove first. Then in Docs go to Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, find Renumberly, and choose Options → Uninstall. To fully remove access, visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
