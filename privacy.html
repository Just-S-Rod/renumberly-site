Renumberly ("the add-on", "we", "us") is a Google Docs™ add-on made by [DEVELOPER NAME]. It numbers your document's headings and shows them in a sidebar. This page explains what it can access and what it does with that access.

The short version

  • The add-on works only with the document you have open while you use it.
  • We don't run a server. The add-on runs inside Google Apps Script.
  • We never collect, store, or see your document's text.
  • We don't ask for your email address or any other Google account information.
  • Nothing leaves Google. The add-on doesn't contact any outside service.
  • We don't sell or share data, and we don't use it for ads.

Permissions the add-on asks for

When you install the add-on, Google asks you to approve these two permissions, and only these two.

Permission (as Google shows it) Why we need it
View and manage documents that this application has been installed in (documents.currentonly) To read the headings in the open document so they can be listed in the sidebar. Also, when you click a button, to add, update, or remove heading numbers. It covers only the document where you use the add-on, not your other files.
Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications (script.container.ui) To add the menu item and show the sidebar.

The add-on can't access your Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, contacts, email address, or any other document.

What happens to your document content

The add-on reads and changes your document only inside Google's systems, while it's running, and only to do what you asked: list headings, jump to a heading, or add or remove numbers. Your content isn't copied to us, stored by us, or sent to anyone else. We have no database and no server.

Settings saved inside Google

The add-on may save small settings in Google's built-in Apps Script storage, such as your numbering preferences, so you don't have to enter them again. This storage is kept by Google and tied to your Google account. We can't read it. (Pending Builder confirmation of exactly what's stored and where.)

Removing the add-on

You can remove the add-on in Docs (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons), or revoke its access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it doesn't delete any heading numbers it added. To take them off first, click Remove in the sidebar.
