Privacy Policy: Renumberly

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Renumberly ("the add-on", "we", "us") is a Google Docs™ add-on made by J. S. Rod. It numbers your document's headings and shows them in a sidebar. This page explains what it can access and what it does with that access.

The short version

  • What it accesses: only the document you have open, and only while the add-on is running. It reads the document's structure to find your headings and their levels.
  • Where it runs: inside Google Apps Script, on Google's servers. We don't send your document content to any server we control, and we don't store your document text.
  • What it stores: your numbering settings and short coded fingerprints of the numbers it added, saved inside that document (details below).
  • What it doesn't do: it doesn't ask for your email address or other Google account information, and it doesn't send your data outside Google's Apps Script environment. We don't sell or share data, use it for ads, or use it to train AI models.

Permissions the add-on asks for

When you install the add-on, Google asks you to approve these two permissions, and only these two.

Permission (as Google shows it) Why we need it
View and manage documents that this application has been installed in (documents.currentonly) To read the headings in the open document so they can be listed in the sidebar. Also, when you click a button, to add, update, or remove heading numbers. It covers only the document where you use the add-on, not your other files.
Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications (script.container.ui) To add the menu item and show the sidebar.

The add-on can't access your Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, contacts, email address, or any other document.

What the add-on accesses

While it's running, the add-on reads the open document's structure to find your headings and their levels. It does this only to do what you ask: list headings, jump to a heading, check heading levels, or add, update, or remove heading numbers.

The add-on runs inside Google Apps Script, on Google's servers. We don't send your document content to any server we control, and we don't store your document text. We have no database and no server of our own.

What the add-on stores

The add-on saves two small things inside the document itself, using Google's built-in Apps Script storage: your numbering settings (such as which heading levels to number), and short coded fingerprints of the numbers it added, so it can update or remove them later. They stay with that document, and we can't read them.

How your data is protected

The add-on runs entirely inside Google Apps Script, on Google's infrastructure. It can only access a document through the Google OAuth permissions you grant. Your document content and the settings saved inside it never leave Google's environment. We have no servers and no database of our own, so we can't see your document contents or those stored settings. If you email support@renumberly.com, that message is kept in our email provider and protected by that account's security.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Numbering settings and fingerprints stay inside the document. Clicking Remove in the sidebar takes the numbers off your headings and clears the fingerprints; your settings stay with the document until you change them or delete the document. Deleting the document deletes everything the add-on stored in it. Revoking access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions stops the add-on from accessing your documents. Support emails are deleted after 90 days, as described above.

Support emails

If you email support@renumberly.com, we use your message and address only to help you. We keep them for 90 days and then delete them, unless the law requires otherwise. Please don't send us your document's contents.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We don't use your data for advertising.
  • We don't use your document content to train AI models.
  • We don't share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and the add-on doesn't send it outside Google's Apps Script environment.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Renumberly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing the add-on

You can remove the add-on in Docs (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons), or revoke its access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it doesn't delete any heading numbers it added. To take them off first, click Remove in the sidebar.

Children

The add-on isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

When this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and post the new version at https://renumberly.com/privacy.html.
